# Guide

Establish a queue with predictable startup and shutdown before publishing and consuming work.

1. [Setup and lifecycle](/libraries/sdq/guide/setup-and-lifecycle): choose storage/ticker and handle recovery and shutdown.
2. [Queue jobs](/libraries/sdq/guide/jobs): use this when reserve, delete, release, and bury transitions need explicit control.
3. [Task API](/libraries/sdq/guide/task-api): use this for JSON payloads, typed handlers, and worker concurrency.

<Callout type="warning" title="Design idempotency before starting workers">
  Reserved jobs can become visible again after a TTR timeout or uncertain process failure. This recovery design also requires handlers to tolerate duplicate execution.
</Callout>
