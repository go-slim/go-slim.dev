# Advanced

Advanced h3 usage is mostly about ownership boundaries:

- who owns route declarations;
- who owns background resources;
- who owns the HTTP server lifecycle;
- who owns protocol-level response behavior.

These topics matter once an application is split into modules or needs tighter production control.
