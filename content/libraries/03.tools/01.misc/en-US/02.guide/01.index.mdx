# Guide

Choose a helper by the concrete operation you need. Keep trust, ownership, and error policy in the application even when the helper makes the operation shorter.

1. [HTTP and client IP](/libraries/misc/guide/http-and-client-ip) covers request inspection and trusted proxy boundaries.
2. [Digests and composition](/libraries/misc/guide/digests-and-composition) covers checksums, password hashing, and sequential functions.
3. [Templates and values](/libraries/misc/guide/templates-and-values) covers interpolation, generic values, and ordered math.

Read [Advanced](/libraries/misc/advanced) before using APIs with aliasing, unsafe, or deprecation concerns.
