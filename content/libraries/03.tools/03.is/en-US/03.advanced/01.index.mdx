# Advanced

Predicates answer narrow questions about values. They do not establish who supplied a value, whether it is authorized, or whether it is safe in another protocol or context.

- [Semantics and security](/libraries/is/advanced/semantics-and-security) documents zero-value behavior, momentary observations, known defects, and trust boundaries.
